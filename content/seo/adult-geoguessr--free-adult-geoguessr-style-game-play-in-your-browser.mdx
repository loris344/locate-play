## Which clues matter when most of the scene is indoors

Since most of these clips are interior, the outdoor clues a Street View player leans on (road signage, vegetation, driving side) mostly aren't available. What replaces them: readable text of any kind, electrical outlet and switch shape, window and shutter construction, and audio when it's usable — an accent or a language on a background TV. Furniture style is the weakest of these on its own, since it travels internationally more than fixtures do.
