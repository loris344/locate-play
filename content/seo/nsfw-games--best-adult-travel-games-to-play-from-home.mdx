## What makes an adult game clever rather than purely explicit

A travel-themed guessing game works because the "destination" framing gives every round a natural narrative — you're not just watching, you're trying to place somewhere on the map, the same instinct that makes real travel photos and postcards interesting. ## Best formats for couples, friends or parties

- **Long-distance couples**: screen-share a session over a call — one person controls the pin, both discuss the guess out loud.
- **Same-room couples**: pass a single device back and forth, alternate who guesses each round.
